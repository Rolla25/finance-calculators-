PRIVACY POLICY

A straightforward view of how Corpusly handles information

Welcome to Corpusly Financial Calculators ("Corpusly", "we", "our", or "us"). This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used and protected when you use our website and financial calculators.

Last updated: June 2026

At a glance

Built to calculate, not to profile you.

  • No sign up or account is required.
  • Calculator inputs are processed in your browser.
  • Anonymous usage data may help us improve the website.
01

Information we collect

Corpusly does not require users to create an account or provide personal information to use our calculators.

We may automatically collect certain non personal information through standard web technologies, including:

  • Browser type and version
  • Device information
  • Operating system
  • Referring website
  • Pages visited
  • Date and time of visits
  • Anonymous usage statistics

This information is collected to improve website performance, functionality and user experience.

02

Cookies

Corpusly may use cookies and similar technologies to enhance user experience, analyse website traffic and improve our services.

Cookies are small files stored on your device that help websites function properly and provide analytical information. You may disable cookies through your browser settings; however, some parts of the website may not function properly if cookies are disabled.

03

Google AdSense

Corpusly may display advertisements provided by Google AdSense and other advertising partners.

Google may use cookies, including the DoubleClick cookie, to serve ads based on your visits to this website and other websites on the internet. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your browsing behaviour.

Manage personalised advertising Read Google's Privacy Policy
04

Analytics

Corpusly may use analytics services such as Google Analytics to understand how visitors interact with the website. Analytics providers may collect information such as:

  • Pages visited
  • Time spent on pages
  • Device information
  • Browser information
  • General geographic location

This information is aggregated and does not personally identify individual users.

05

Financial calculator disclaimer

The calculators and tools provided on Corpusly are for informational and educational purposes only.

i

Results generated by our calculators are estimates based on the information entered by users and should not be considered financial, investment, tax, insurance or legal advice. Users should consult qualified professionals before making financial decisions.

06

Data security

We take reasonable measures to protect our website and services. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

07

Third party links

Our website may contain links to third party websites for informational purposes. We are not responsible for the privacy practices, content or policies of third party websites.

Users are encouraged to review the privacy policies of any external websites they visit.

08

Children's privacy

Corpusly is not directed toward children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us and we will take appropriate action.

09

Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be posted on this page along with a revised “Last updated” date.

Continued use of the website after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated Privacy Policy.

Questions about this policy?

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, you may contact us through the contact information provided on the website.
