Standard EMI formula

The regular EMI uses the reducing balance method with a monthly interest rate. Pre EMI interest is calculated separately using actual days and a 365 day year.

EMI = P × r × (1 + r)ⁿ ÷ ((1 + r)ⁿ − 1)
Advanced EMI questions, answered clearly
How is the EMI calculated?
The calculator uses the loan amount, annual interest rate and repayment tenure to estimate the EMI using the reducing balance method.
What is pre EMI interest?
It is the interest accrued between the loan disbursement date and the first EMI date. The calculator collects it through the first installment and carries forward any unpaid amount.
How does a prepayment affect my loan?
A prepayment reduces the outstanding principal. You can use it either to lower your future EMI or shorten the loan tenure.
What is a step up EMI?
A step up EMI increases your repayment periodically by a percentage, fixed monthly amount or both. This can help repay the loan sooner and reduce total interest.
Why might the result differ from my lender's schedule?
Lenders may use different rounding rules, day count methods, fees and prepayment policies. The calculator's results are estimates and should be confirmed with your lender.
Can I add multiple prepayments?
Yes. You can enter multiple prepayments with different dates, amounts and actions. They are processed chronologically.
What is the difference between "Reduce EMI" and "Reduce Tenure"?
"Reduce EMI" recalculates a lower EMI over the remaining repayment period. "Reduce Tenure" retains the current EMI and uses the lower principal balance to repay the loan sooner.
Does the loan tenure begin from the disbursement date?
The entered tenure is the number of scheduled monthly repayments beginning on the first EMI date. The gap before that date affects pre EMI interest but does not silently remove installments from the tenure.
Why does the first EMI date affect my total repayment?
A longer gap between disbursement and the first EMI creates more accrued interest. The calculator collects that amount through the first installment and carries forward any unpaid portion without charging it twice.
!

This calculator provides estimates for educational and planning purposes only. Actual lender schedules may differ because of rounding, fees, day count conventions, rate changes and prepayment rules. Confirm repayment terms and charges with your lender before making a financial decision.
