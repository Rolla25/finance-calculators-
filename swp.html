SWP PLANNING, EXPLAINED

How to use this SWP calculator

Enter your corpus, expected return and preferred withdrawal approach. The calculator applies monthly compounding and shows each withdrawal so you can review the complete projection.

Plan monthly income

Use fixed duration mode to estimate a starting monthly withdrawal designed to use the corpus over your selected period.

Test corpus longevity

Use fixed withdrawal mode to estimate how long the corpus could support the monthly income you choose.

Account for inflation

Increase withdrawals once each year to model income that aims to maintain purchasing power over time.

Important Disclaimer

This calculator provides educational estimates based on a constant return assumption. Actual corpus longevity may differ due to market volatility, inflation, taxes, expenses and withdrawal changes. Results are not guaranteed and should not be treated as financial advice.
