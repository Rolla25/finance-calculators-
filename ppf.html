UNDERSTAND THE ESTIMATE

How this PPF calculation works

The calculator starts with your current balance, adds each planned annual contribution and credits interest at the end of every projected financial year.

Deposit timing

PPF interest uses the lowest monthly balance between the close of the fifth day and month end. This projection assumes your annual deposit is available by 5 April, so it receives a full year of interest.

Maturity period

A PPF account matures after fifteen complete financial years from the end of the financial year in which it was opened. Enter only completed account years when using an existing balance.

Inflation adjustment

The value in today's money discounts the projected corpus by your inflation assumption. It does not change the nominal PPF interest calculation.

Calculation basis

For each projected year, the eligible opening balance and deposit earn the assumed annual rate. Actual PPF interest is credited annually and future rates may change.

Closing balance = Opening balance + Deposit + ((Opening balance + Deposit) × Interest rate)

Rules reference: Public Provident Fund Scheme, 2019.
