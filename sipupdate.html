SIP INVESTING, EXPLAINED

How to use this SIP calculator

Enter your monthly investment, expected annual return and investment period to estimate your future mutual fund corpus. Use Goal Planner when you know the amount you want to reach and need to calculate the monthly SIP required.

Calculate future SIP returns

See your estimated corpus, total contribution and potential wealth generated from returns. Add an existing lump sum for a combined projection.

Compare a Step Up SIP

Increase the contribution each year to model income growth and see how a yearly step up may improve the final value.

Plan with inflation

View the purchasing power of your projected corpus in today's rupees, or inflate a financial goal to estimate its future cost.

How the calculation works

The projection uses monthly compounding and assumes each SIP contribution is invested at the beginning of the month. Results are estimates. Mutual fund returns are market linked and not guaranteed.
